Core risks

Serious product. Serious boundaries.

LedgerLayer wins by handling difficult trust problems cleanly, not by pretending they do not exist.

  • Keep summaries and workflow clearly distinct from regulated financial advice.
  • Use principal firms, legal review, and tightly scoped launch claims.
  • Never market calculation outputs as substitute professional advice.
  • No data monetisation. No resale. No hidden referral economics.
  • UK-first hosting, encryption, audit trails, and least-privilege access.
  • Make access and document history legible to the household.
  • Manual onboarding early. Automation later.
  • Human review for sensitive document categories.
  • Operational quality matters more than launch theatrics.
  • Complex does not mean cluttered.
  • Premium users want depth, but they still need hierarchy.
  • The interface should feel dense, calm, and fully in control.
